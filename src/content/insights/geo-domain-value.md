Invented brands start at zero. Geographic domains start with a place people already search for.

## Intent is pre-loaded

When someone types *Central Phoenix real estate* or *Central Phoenix restaurants*, the geography is the query. A hostname that contains the geography does not have to earn relevance from scratch — it inherits it from the phrase residents already use.

Invented names reverse the work: every mention has to carry a subtitle, a logo, and a "what do they do?" until the brand catches up.

## Three properties of a strong geo domain

1. **The phrase is stable.** City and district names outlast slogan cycles. Central Phoenix was the core yesterday and will be the core next decade.
2. **The code is shared.** PHX compresses city-wide identity into three letters — airport, weather, listings — without narrowing the category.
3. **The length is printable.** Six letters fit on a yard sign, a van door, and a maps pin. Short geo names survive every channel.
